Methodology

How we evaluate software.

Our unit of analysis is not “the best product.” It is a buyer decision in a specific market.

Evidence hierarchy

  1. Official product, pricing, help, security and legal pages.
  2. Official app marketplaces and partner documentation.
  3. Independent market observation for search intent and comparison structure.
  4. Inference only when clearly labeled.

What can change a recommendation

Locale

Currency, tax rules, payment methods, language support and local infrastructure.

Workflow

Integrations, migration path, team size, implementation burden and support.

Commercial fit

Pricing model, plan limits, renewal cost and whether the buyer can self-serve.

Affiliate relationships

Affiliate economics are tracked separately from product fit. A higher commission does not improve an editorial score.
