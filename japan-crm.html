Small-business CRM in Japan: start with LINE, support, and implementation path.

A global CRM ranking misses several Japanese decision variables. For a small team, LINE integration, JPY pricing, Japanese support, and whether the purchase remains self-service can matter more than feature count.

Use-case split

Choose a LINE-first comparison when customer conversations are central to the CRM workflow. Choose a platform-growth comparison when marketing, sales, service and content tooling may expand around the CRM.

Important: Subsidy eligibility and affiliate attribution are separate issues. A subsidy-assisted procurement path should not be assumed to behave like a normal self-service affiliate purchase.
